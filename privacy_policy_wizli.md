# Privacy Policy for Wizli

Welcome to Wizli! Your privacy and the privacy of our young learners are of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Wizli mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect
We aim to collect as little personal information as possible to provide a seamless educational experience.

*   **Account Information:** Currently, Wizli operates without requiring an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
*   **Usage Data:** We may collect anonymous, aggregated data on how the App is used (e.g., which subjects are most popular or app performance metrics) to help us improve the learning experience.
*   **User Inputs (Questions & Chats):** The questions you type into the App are processed by our AI backend (e.g., Google Gemini) solely for the purpose of generating educational answers and explanations. 

## 2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Wizli is designed to be an educational tool for students, including children under the age of 13. 
*   We **do not** knowingly collect personal identifiable information (PII) from children.
*   We strongly advise users not to enter personal information (like names, addresses, or school names) into the chat interface.
*   If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it immediately.

## 3. How We Use Information
The data we process is used exclusively to:
*   Provide real-time AI-powered answers and educational explanations.
*   Maintain and improve the App's performance and features.
*   Track learning streaks and basic app state locally on your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Wizli utilizes third-party AI services to generate responses. Your chat inputs are sent to these services (e.g., Google) for processing. These third-party services are bound by their own privacy policies and are restricted from using this data to identify individual users. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.

## 5. Data Storage & Security
*   **Local Storage:** Your learning streak, app preferences (like Dark Mode), and quiz progress are stored locally on your device.
*   **Security:** We use standard security protocols to ensure any data transmitted between the App and our AI servers is encrypted and secure.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify users through the App. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
